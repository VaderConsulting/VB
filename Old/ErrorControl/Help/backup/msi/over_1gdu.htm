Microsoft Windows Installer

Overview of Windows Installer

The Microsoft® Windows® Installer reduces the total cost of ownership (TCO) for your customers by enabling them to efficiently install and configure your products and applications. The installer can also provide your product with new capabilities to advertise features without installing them, to install products on demand, and to add user customizations.
